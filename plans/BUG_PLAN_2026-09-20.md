## Engine bugs

### BUG-01 — Action agents are never offered. **DONE** `f28dcf1` + `d878701`

The engine prints "After a player activates a system:" for `l1z1xagent` and then never opens that window,
so the leader is unreachable in play. Tracked since 2026-09-04 in
`plans/BUG_2026-09-04_LEADER_USE_UNREACHABLE.md`; the fix belongs to **LEADER-FIX-002** in
`plans/LEADER-FIX-2026-09-13.md` (reactive combat/activation/production windows). The implementation arm
at `leaders.rs:1324` looks correct — it is simply never reached, because nothing raises the activation
window. This is the delivery half of the leader problem and the largest engine item on the list.

*To do:* failing test — ready `l1z1xagent`, activate a system, assert the agent component action is offered
and settles an infantry-for-mech swap through the normal ask/settle path; refusals carry reasons.

### BUG-02 — Guild Ships: offered, then refused. **DONE** `09409d7`

Generation asked `transactions::partners` (which honours `faction_abilities::ignores_neighbours`), legality
asked bare `are_neighbours`. A Hacan seat was offered a partner across the board and refused the moment it
accepted — a legal action rejected late, indistinguishable from a dead button. `transactions::may_transact`
now answers reach the way generation does. 1343 engine lib tests pass, 1352 across all targets.

### BUG-03 — Note for note is not a shape the engine generates. **TODO, frontier review first**

`transactions::offer_options` (transactions.rs:763) is the single list of what may go on a table, and the
diplomatic contact derives from it via `trade_bundles`, so the gap is in one place. The list has the
Support-for-the-Throne swap — which needs a Support note in **both** hands, hence "not possible with Hacan
first round" — and a note sold for trade goods. Nothing puts one note against another note, so "my
Mercantism for your Ceasefire" is never offered for any faction. The contact's own note template narrows
notes twice more: `owner_of(note)` must be your own faction (a note you were given cannot be passed on) and
`.take(3)`.

*To do:* add the note-for-note shape, priced through the existing `note_cost` (three quarters of worth;
Support gives up a victory point), with the id grammar `offer_from` parses, and `can_pay`'s note rules
satisfying both sides. **Not to be started without a frontier review**: notes are payments, and a wrong
price settles silently instead of refusing, which is the failure mode this project cannot detect after the
fact. Both windows inherit the fix, since both read the one list.

### BUG-04 — With diplomacy on, the transaction window is gone. **ASK**

`transactions::available_actions` returns nothing when `state.diplomacy.enabled`, and two tests assert it
(`diplomacy/candidates.rs:1201`, `:1252`). The intent is "trading happens inside the contact", and the
contact's trade generator does derive from the legacy shapes — but the operator enabled diplomacy in the new
setup form and lost a capability they had with it off. Three answers, and the choice changes table
behaviour, so it is not mine to take:

- **A** — make contact trading complete (BUG-03, plus BUG-01's window delivery). Correct, largest, and the
  `DIPLO-001` in `plans/ENGINE_DIPLOMACY_UNIFICATION.md`.
- **B** — keep legacy transactions available while diplomacy is on. An hour, restores capability at once;
  it deliberately breaks two passing tests and puts two negotiation windows on the table, which is the
  duplication the unification exists to remove.
- **C** — nothing: leave diplomacy off and Hacan trades normally, Guild Ships and all.

### BUG-05 / BUG-06 — techs offered without prerequisites. **BLOCKED on the saved game**

The decidable half is decided and now guarded permanently: `technology::tests` proves a seat with no
technologies, no specialties and no laws is offered only the prerequisite-free corpus — and asserts the list
is not empty, so the test cannot pass by offering nothing (`cf14895`). Therefore both reports depend on
what was in that table: a planetary specialty stands in for one prerequisite of its colour (90.8), Research
Team laws and Prophet's Tears each spend an "ignore 1 prerequisite" budget, Jol-Nar's Analytical waives one,
and Space Dock II against one yellow plus one waiver is legal while against zero yellow it is not.

*To do:* the `.ti4replay` (or recording directory) plus, per item, seat, round and what was expected. Then
a failing test at that frame.

### BUG-08 — Refresh was offered, paying the due was not. **PARTIAL**

A due with too few trade goods has at least two legal answers — pay what you can, or refresh — and the seat
was offered one. That is the project's own definition of a bug: legal actions are generated, not accepted by
late rejection. It is also the operator losing an option rather than gaining a stranger one, which is why it
sits first. *To do:* reproduce a due on a short seat and assert both options are present.

### BUG-09 — Warfare asks production before the strategy spend. **TODO**

Spending from the strategy card changes the production budget, so asking production first either wastes the
spend or records a produced set that did not exist. *To do:* a test pinning the current ask order (so the
change is visible), then move the spend ask ahead of the production ask in the invasion sequence.

## Viewer work

### UI-01 — Exploration cards have to be explicitly resolved. **TODO, shape decision first**

`[verified]` Neither viewer mentions exploration at all. The biggest viewer item here, and it starts with a
question I should not answer alone: show the recorded **draws** (which card, what was gained), a per-card
**state** (unexplored / revealed / resolved / refused), or a **pending** lane for anything the engine asked
and no seat answered. The first is presentation of facts already in the frame; the third is a new concept
and would be the honest home for BUG-08's symptom too. Start with the first.

### UI-02 — Seats are named. **DONE** `bfa91e4`

`view::seat_name` gives `seat2 (The Emirates of Hacan)`, falling back to `seat3 (unknown_faction_id)` rather
than inventing a friendly name for a faction the content store does not know.

### UI-03 — Attachments. **PARTIAL — blocked on the content corpus**

Names now print instead of `attachment_fragment_count`. That is the last honest thing the view layer can do:
**all 22 records in `crates/ti4-content/content/attachments.json` carry only `id`, `name`, `source`,
`techSpeciality` — there is no rules text anywhere in the corpus.** *To do:* add the text field, sourced
from the official rules, then an engine test that each attachment does something — which is the half of
"maybe attachments are broken" nobody can answer from a name.

### UI-04 — Diplomacy tooltips need the actual terms. **DONE** `847548b` (terms on the row; no valuation)

`Terms::describe` already renders a spoken leg ("3 trade goods, Mercantism, a promissory note from the
Arbiter") and the frame's action summary already carries terms. Wiring, not design. Until it exists every
diplomacy question is unanswerable after the fact.

### UI-05 — Probabilities for the player who is choosing. **TODO, with a rule**

The plumbing exists (`p-choose` surfaces policy scores for diagnosis). The rule, and it belongs in the UI
string and not only in this plan: **name the quantity** — "policy preference out of 1000", "softmax share of
the offered options", "rollouts this seat won out of N" — never print one as a win chance unless rollouts
produced it, and keep the number out of game state so it cannot leak into a save or influence a choice.
`plans/DIPLOMACY_HONESTY.md` exists because a number that looks like a chance gets trusted like one.

### UI-06 — Planet rider totals. **TODO, and the safest thing on this list**

Refreshed and exhausted **resource and influence totals** next to the planet count, plus the **trait** and
**tech specialty** breakdown. Pure presentation of state the engine already holds, no rules surface to get
wrong, and it answers questions the map cannot.

### NOTE-01 — Transactions during the action phase. **NOTED, not scheduled**

Understood as hard, and recorded as a constraint on BUG-04 and DIPLO-001: neither may be sold as making
action-phase trading easy. One adjacent question stays open deliberately rather than guessed at: the
per-partner limit is enforced in generation (`state.transacted_with`) and
`transactions::neighbours_who_transacted` (transactions.rs:210) counts only neighbours, so whether a
Guild-Ships partner counts toward a limit worded about neighbours is LRR 60's question, not mine.
