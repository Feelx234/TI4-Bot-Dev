# BF-firmament (The Firmament, alias `firmament`) -- part A of Firmament / Obsidian

Code: `factions/firmament.rs` (plot model use, Plots Within Plots, Planesplitter, Neural Parasite, Heaven's Eye, Viper EX-23, Myru Vos, Captain Aroz, Sharsiss, Black Ops, The Sowing), `factions/firmament_flip.rs` (Puppets of the Blade and the typed transition to The Obsidian), `ti4-model/src/plots.rs` (the `Plot` type). Ledger: firmament 11/11 implemented; every other line unchanged.

## Part A / part B split

* **Part A (this package)**: every asset on the `firmament` sheet (2 abilities, 2 technologies, flagship, mech, 3 leaders, Black Ops, The Sowing, home 96a with Cronos and Tallin) plus the shared flip, `firmament_flip::become_obsidian`.
* **Part B (not claimed, not in `MODULES`)**: the Obsidian sheet (`nocturne`, `bladesorchestra`, `marionettes`), `planesplitter-obs`, `parasite-obs`, `obsidian_flagship`, `obsidian_mech`, `obsidianagent/commander/hero`, `malevolency`, `obsidianbt`, and the plot cards' own effects (Enervate, Siphon, Seethe, Assail, Extract and their mutated sides in `genericcards.json`).
* What the flip already does that is Obsidian text: The Blade's Orchestra's "flip your home system, double-sided faction components and all in-play plot cards; ready Cronos Hollow and Tallin Hollow if you control them" is the body of the transition (it has to run when the faction changes). The ability id `bladesorchestra` is **not** claimed; part B claims it and tests it as its own row.
* **Hooks left for part B**: `GameState` faction is `obsidian` and its leaders, note (`malevolency:obsidian`), technologies (`*-obs`), breakthrough (`obsidianbt`) and units (`obsidian_*`) are already in place on the seat; the plot cards are `Plot` values (faceup) with their tokens, so "puppeted players" is `firmament::puppeted(state, seat)`; the goods on The Reaping are `firmament::goods_on_card`; the staged event `FACTION_FLIPPED` (`player`, `from`, `to`) is announced after the action, which is where the Viper Hollow's "if this unit was coexisting when this card flipped" hangs; `supply::staging_enabled` stays true for an `obsidian` seat. Part B only adds a module for `obsidian` (abilities, cards, hooks); it does not change the transition.

## Model decisions

* **Plots**: `Player::plots` (an existing, unused `Vec<String>`) now holds one `ti4_model::plots::Plot` per card (`d:<tokens>` facedown, `u:<tokens>` faceup; the original bare one-token string still reads as facedown). A card can carry several tokens, never two of one player's. `Player::plot_objectives` (existing) holds the secrets scored as plots. The schema is unchanged. A plot's own identity (Enervate etc.) is not modelled: no Firmament text needs it, and part B can add a field to the encoding.
* **Hidden information**: `ti4_model::view::redact_player_with` replaces another player's facedown cards with `?` (count survives); `view::leaks` reports an unredacted facedown card. Faceup (flipped) cards are public. The engine reads the real state, so Heaven's Eye and the hero know whom they puppet.
* **Plots Within Plots**: a scoring option `plot|<secret>|<player>` is added by `firmament::plot_options` to the same `ScoringWindow` choice (no new ask site), for every secret another player has scored (and that has not become a public objective) whose requirement the Firmament meets at that timing (`secrets::fulfils`: status by position; action/agenda by a feat for this occurrence or by position, exactly as for held secrets). Scoring pays the secret's price (`pay_for`), records the score (`record_score`, so it counts as a scored objective for the hero's three), records the plot objective, gives no VP and places a facedown plot with that player's token. It is exempt from the status window's one-secret cap and from the event windows' `OnePerPlayer` cap (the player stays in the window while a plot is on offer), and `secrets::scored_count` excludes it (the hand limit). If two other players scored the same secret there are two options (the choice is the token).
* **Flip** (`firmament_flip::become_obsidian`, atomic on a clone): faction to `obsidian`; home tile 96a to 96b through `movement::apply_map_edit` (recorded in `faction_marks`, replayed by `Game` each step), ships and command tokens carried by it, each planet's control, ground forces, coexistence, attachments and exhaustion carried by the transition (cronos to cronoshollow, tallin to tallinhollow by sheet position), controlled Hollows readied; the three leaders swapped (agent readied, commander and hero locked); note ids renamed from `:firmament` to `:obsidian` (the seat's generic notes wherever they are), Black Ops purged everywhere, `malevolency:obsidian` dealt; units renamed; owned `*-firm` technologies become `*-obs` with exhaustion kept; The Sowing becomes The Reaping with its goods; plots flipped faceup; borrowed `firmamentcommander` grants dropped; the `private:#seen:*` marks follow so the swap is not announced as gaining a technology/breakthrough; `FACTION_FLIPPED` staged. Offered through `mapped_component_actions` (needs the map for the tile). `supply::staging_enabled` also holds for an `obsidian` seat.
* **Heaven's Eye**: `MovementHooks::move_bonus` (+1 when the active system holds units, in space or on planets, of a player with a token on one of the owner's plots) and two mandatory `after` abilities on `SPACE_COMBAT_ROUND_ENDED` / `GROUND_COMBAT_ROUND_ENDED` that repair it when the same condition holds; Nekro Z lends the text only (a Nekro has no plots, so it is inert).
* **Viper EX-23**: `invasion.rs` offers the existing "fight or coexist" question (same option ids and decision subtype) for a committed Viper (invader) and to the Viper's owner as defender, naming the card; it uses the same `deepwrought::begin_coexisting`, the same guards (a coexisting invader starts no combat and takes no control) and the same 3.1/3.2 control reading as Research Team on defense.
* **Myru Vos**: optional `after` ability on `SYSTEM_ACTIVATED` (the moment before the activating player moves; owner may exhaust it), mark `firmament:agent` = `<activation_seq>|<mover>`. SPACE CANNON: `combat::space_cannon_offense` treats it like Solar Flare (every other player's guns are silenced against the mover for that activation). Pass-through: new `MovementHooks::unladen_pass`; `MovementRules::path_from_ship` searches with the ordinary rules first and only if no route exists searches again without 58.4b; `CargoWindow::for_ship` loads nothing onto a ship whose route crosses such a system (`firmament::unladen_route_forbids_cargo`: the agent covers the activation, no other rule lets the ship pass, an intermediate system holds another player's ships that do not allow it). A ship that can sail clear keeps its cargo.
* **Planesplitter**: `fracture::enter_play_by_effect` (rule 11: the gainer chooses one specialty planet per colour, then the ordinary entry), on `TECHNOLOGY_GAINED` for the owner of the card.
* **Black Ops**: resolved by an ability on `TRANSACTION_RESOLVED` for the Firmament seat when its note is held by another seat: the Firmament is asked (place a facedown plot with the holder's token, or decline), then the holder gains 2 command tokens (asked per token, existing `gain_tokens`) and 2 trade goods, and the note is purged.
* **Sharsiss**: `leader_action`/`use_leader` hooks (generic caller purges). Step 1 asked only when several other players exist.
* **The Sowing**: one question (1..=min(3, trade goods), or decline) at `BREAKTHROUGH_GAINED` and `STATUS_PHASE_BEGAN`; the goods leave the player's trade goods and are the mark `firmament:sowing:<player>` (not spendable, not in supply).

## Open questions and limits

1. **Sharsiss, first sentence**: "Place 1 of your plot cards in play with any other player's control token on it" is read as placing a plot card from the owner's supply into play with another player's token on it. The alternative (moving an existing plot) has nowhere to put it.
2. **Facedown visibility**: the card text does not say who may read the tokens on a facedown plot. Implemented as: only the owner while facedown; everyone after the flip.
3. **Technologies on the flip**: owned `*-firm` cards flip; unowned ones are not gained ("gain all of the faction components" read as the components that exist; both `*-obs` technologies say "cannot be researched").
4. **Myru Vos**: "those ships" is read as all of the mover's ships (as Solar Flare); the pass-through is offered only when no clear route exists and then bars cargo for that move; the effect starts at the activation window, not at a per-ship moment. "When a player moves ships" is the activation (there is no earlier window).
5. **Black Ops** is resolved at the transaction that moved it; a note reaching a hand any other way (none exists today) would wait for the next `TRANSACTION_RESOLVED`.
6. **Plot supply** is unlimited (no count is printed).
7. **Plots Within Plots and public objectives**: a secret turned into a public objective is not offered as a plot. A secret scored as a plot counts as a scored objective (hero unlock, scored lists) but not toward the secret limit.
8. **Planesplitter** enters the Fracture without emitting `FRACTURE_ENTERED_PLAY` (the timing window has no emitter); Nekro-assimilated copies are not "gained".
9. **Heaven's Eye repair** is read to include ground combat rounds in the system.
10. **Flip bookkeeping**: Valefar tokens or marks naming the Firmament sheet, and any mark keyed by the string `firmament` other than notes and the commander grant, are not rewritten.
11. **Soak** does not by itself prove plot scoring or the flip occur in random play; the tests above drive them.

### Design notes (2026-10-08)

* **Plots Within Plots flow**: option `plot|<secret>|<player>` (offered only while a plot card remains and the secret is not already a plot of this Firmament) pays the price and records `plot_objectives`; `ScoringWindow` then keeps a pending `plot_card` step and asks the Firmament which of the remaining cards to place (`DecisionSource::FactionAbility("plotsplots")`, subtype `plot_card`, one question only when several remain). `ScoringWindow::is_complete` waits for it. The scoring `Choice::new` count in `objectives.rs::pending_choice` is 2 (registered in `decision_delivery_inventory.rs`).
* **Reveal**: `firmament_flip::become_obsidian` stays atomic and typed; it flips the plots and stages `FACTION_FLIPPED`; the "when this card is revealed" effects answer that event (`firmament_plots`).
* **Schema**: `Player::plots` / `plot_objectives` keep their types (strings / set); the stored string gained the card name and the old forms still read, so no schema envelope change. Documents written before this change load with plots of unknown identity (no effect, they occupy one of the five places).
* **Superseded open questions**: 1 (Sharsiss reading is now the ruled one: a new card from the remaining five); 2 (tokens on a facedown plot are public, only the card identity is hidden); 4 (Myru Vos covers only the ships that move); 6 (the plot supply is five cards); 7 (a plot-scored secret is not a scored objective).

### Open questions (2026-10-08)

1. **Myru Vos, mixed fleets**: units are interchangeable values with no identity, so if the mover has a ship in the active system that did not move, SPACE CANNON fires and its hits are assigned as the mover chooses (a covered ship could be hit). Holding the covered ships out of hit assignment would need unit identity.
2. **Plots Within Plots and the price**: the secret's price is paid when the secret is chosen, before the plot card is chosen (the card question cannot fail).
3. **No plot card remaining**: Plots Within Plots offers no plot option at all (it would score nothing); Black Ops skips its "may place" question but still pays the holder; Sharsiss skips its first sentence.
4. **Legacy stored plots** (no card name) are never "revealed" and have no effect.
