## Review concern at c5632448 and resolution

`status::resolve_before_token_gain_with` removes action cards from the deck and adds them to a hand before calling the Yssaril Scheming discard hook. If the decider refuses or gives an illegal choice, the function returns an error with mutated state. A rollback of the draw/hand and staged marks, plus a negative test with an invalid discard answer, is needed before claiming the new live status route atomic. This concern applies to the new faction, not an original six-faction game. Resolved in `c79d38f3`: the complete status state is restored on an illegal Scheming discard, with a red/green regression test and independent Terra review. Exact checks and the one missing-fixture simulator failure are recorded in `plans/evidence/BF-STATUS-DRAW-ATOMIC.md`. The behavior table above remains the comparison for the prior clean commit `c5632448`; this error-path-only fix does not change those measured successful games.
