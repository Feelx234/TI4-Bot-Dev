## Remaining, with the precise reason

Action cards played inside another area's window. Only the cards with a REACTION timing can be played from a window at all (`window_of(timing)`);
Ghost Ship, Mercenary/Pirate Contract, Pirate Fleet, Jam, Refit Troops, Brilliance, Impersonation, Plagiarize, Focused Research, Divert Funding,
Exploration Probe and Strategize/Overrule have an Action timing (a component action): no host window ever plays them, so `Fx::nested` is moot
for them (they run in their own announce window with their own facts) and they are not bails. The nested ones:

* **Courageous To The End** (`SHIP_DESTROYED after`): the facts are handed in now (`SharedPlay::{courageous_subtype, courageous_cause, hits}`), the hosts are lent the stream (the composed machine). The lockstep found two differences, both in the dual adapter and ledger, not the rules: (1) `nested.ability_dice`: the card's two dice are not recorded as `RollPurpose::Ability` ledger rows (the string compares them), (2) the opponent's casualty question has no display context in the core (the string's has `hit{cause: courageous_to_the_end, producer: holder}`, phase `strategy`, `battle=false`): the `courageous_casualty` branch of `drive_windowed` is written but the context is `<absent>` on the core side (the `complete_nested` path takes it). Left a counted bail again (`Fx::Courageous` stays out of `nested()`), test `bail_courageous_to_the_end_is_counted`.
* **Scramble Frequency** and **Fire Team**: registered plays of their host window (Fire Team in the ground roll tail, ported). Scramble needs (a) a registered `scramble` play in `ti4_engine_core_factions::cards` (redraw all dice of `last_reroll_player`'s staged set, forced, no question; the kind check is redundant in the core: only the barrage and the space cannon offense emit `UNIT_ABILITY_ROLLED`), (b) the hosts to stage their dice before the event and re-read them after it: the barrage (`finish_side`: `mirror` before the emit, read the faces back, recompute the hits, `unmirror`; set `last_reroll_player`), the space cannon offense (`roll`/`reroll_windows`: no staging at all today; the staged set per gunner from the plan) and the bombardment (does not emit the event in the core), (c) the redraws recorded in `drawn`/the ledger the way the string's `apply_reroll_dice` records them. Not started.
* **Upgrade** (`SYSTEM_ACTIVATED after`), **Decoy Operation**, **Harness Energy**, **Infiltrate**, **Bribery**, **Lie in Wait**, **Crash Landing**, **Experimental Battlestation**: their hosts are windows of the tactical action, the vote and the transaction (not ported or not lent the stream and the map); the facts they need (`SeatPlayMore`, `SharedPlay`) are handed in, except the neighbours who traded (Lie in Wait: from the map) and the galaxy (Battlestation); `reaction_can_matter` answers `Unknown::HarnessOrInfiltrate` and `Unknown::BriberyGoods` for three of them (the core has no judge of those costs: the adapter would hand in the seat's potential goods and the planet facts). Each stays a counted bail named by its card.
