# web2: local play, transport, drafts, saved game

Part of `web2/AGENTS.md`: the Patterns and the rules of Screen size and Text there apply here too.

## Local play

- `?local=<seed>&players=8&humans=<mask>` plays a real game of the engine in the page (`src/dev/LocalApp.tsx`). Build the engine first: `scripts/build-wasm.sh`.
- "Single player" of the start screen is `?single`: the setup of a table in the page, and its game (the Lobby section of `lobby.md`). `?local=…` and `?scenario=…` stay the games of a fixed setup, for tests and for the tools of the Dev sheet. `DEFAULT_GAME` (seed 42, 8 seats, seat A played here; `src/dev/defaultGame.ts`) was single player until phase 6 of the lobby plan, and the bare address until 2026-10-13: what it saved is kept under the same key, and a browser that has only that game opens it from "Single player". The installed app opens `/`, the start screen. `?example=…` is the demo and `?gallery` the gallery.
- If the engine of a local game does not load (missing file, or no JSPI), the demo opens with the reason in a line above the shell.
- The header has a link to the other app: "Demo" in a local game, "Local game" in the demo. On desktop it is a button after the reference sheets; on a phone it is the last item of "Reference and more". A page load, not a game choice.
- A local game has no bar at the bottom. "Dev" (a header button on desktop; the item after the other-app item in "Reference and more" on a phone) opens the Dev sheet, which is a reference sheet: the seed, the seats, the saved answers, the other seats, "Play as", "Start from", New game, Export and Import. It was "Settings" until 2026-10-10: everything in it is for testing. Without the shell (loading, a saved game of another engine, a notice) the page has a row with only "Dev". The demo has its own bar and no Dev sheet.
- "Start from" is one list: "Round 1" and the scenarios of the engine (`crates/ti4-host/src/view/scenario.rs`). A row is the control and loads the page at the address of that game (`?scenario=<id>&humans=<mask>`); what a scenario holds is behind "?". A scenario is a game of its own: the engine sets up its board on a fixed seed, and its answers are saved under its own key, so a reload, an undo and New game work as in any game. A new case for testing is added to a scenario in Rust, with a test that the facts show it. A new feature gets its cases in the late-game scenarios when it can (owner, 2026-10-11), so that it is seen in a full game and not in round 1 only: every state that its screen shows, at every seat. Example: for the Secrets sheet every seat has three secret objectives, some scored, some that are scored in the action phase and some of the other phases.
- `src/session/` turns an update of the game into the view models. It imports `model` only, and it decides no rule: a value that needs a rule comes from the engine.
- The page does not know where the game runs. `Transport` (`src/session/transport.ts`) is the seam: an update, an answer, a script that is run, a plan or a draft that is sent, the mode of a card, an undo. A tool for testing a local game (Step, "Play as", the scenarios, the saved game) is a capability of the transport (`Transport.capabilities`, `GameSession.capabilities`): the Dev entry and each part of the Dev sheet are shown from it, never from the kind of game. How a plan answers a question of the game is decided in Rust (`script_answer` in `ti4-host`); the wasm host has an export for it (`ti4_plan`).
- A decision without a dedicated screen is one list in the action panel.
- The activation and the movement of a tactical action use the screens of these steps. In the movement the systems that ships can leave are the choice on the board, as in the demo, so a phone stays on the map. The facts come from the engine with the update (`tactical`), and "Move fleet" sends the staged movement as one plan (`Transport.submitPlan`). A plan that the game refuses is taken back whole; one undo takes a movement back whole, and it is staged again as it was sent, so the player can change a part of it. The check marks that the player set by hand are not kept.
- The landings of an invasion use the screen of that step too (the Invasion section of `tactical.md`). The facts are the third kind of `tactical`, and "Commit ground forces" sends the staged landings as one plan, with the rules of a movement plan: refused whole, stopped by another decision with the rest staged again, and taken back whole by one undo. The staged landings are kept for a reload as the staged movement is. After the last unit the game does not ask for the end of the landings, so the plan needs no answer for it.
- The production uses the screen of that step too (the Production section of `tactical.md`). The facts are the fourth kind of `tactical` (`ProductionFacts`): every unit of a production with its price, its places, its supply and its printed values, and why the seat cannot produce it when it cannot, the limits, the discount, and the planets and trade goods that pay. "Produce units" sends the build and its payment as one plan, with the rules of a movement plan. The game asks for a payment after each purchase; the plan answers with the staged payments in their order, whenever it is asked, and leaves out what the game did not need. A build whose payment does not cover it is refused whole. The staged production is kept for a reload.
- The limits of a staged production are sums over the facts: the fleet pool and the capacity do not count what a rule excuses (fighters at a space dock, Fighter II). The game has the last word when the plan is sent.
- The Draft tab opens the private draft of a tactical action: its activation, its movement, its landings and its production, on the screens of these steps (pattern 10). A draft is a script (`src/session/draft.ts`): the tactical action, its system, then the steps of its movement, of its landings and of its production. The engine plays the script as the next turn of the seat on a copy of the game, each time the draft or the game changes (`Transport.draft`), and the screens are made from where that run ended. The answer comes later than the question (a `draft` event), also in a local game: until it is there, the screen is of the draft before and its main button waits. A change of the game alone (another seat acted) does not make it wait: the screen and what is staged stay, and the answer for the new game replaces it. What is staged is cleared only by a new question of the viewer (`fresh` in `useLiveSession.ts`), in Live and in both drafts. Nothing of it is in the game or in its saved decisions.
- The draft opens whenever the page waits in the strategy phase or the action phase and the seat has not passed; the tab says why when it does not. In the strategy phase it is the draft of the turn that comes (owner, 2026-10-10). The action menu stays a list: a tactical action chosen there is played live.
- In the draft, "Activate system" records the system, and every change of the movement is recorded at once (the Movement section of `tactical.md`). Undo and Redo of the toolbar go through the states of the draft; "Start over" and "Discard draft" are in its menu. The draft and its history are kept (`SeatStore`, slot "plan": `planKey` in `localStorage` in a local game, the server online), so a reload keeps them.
- "Apply to Live" is enabled at the turn menu of the seat, when the game played the whole draft. Its dialog says what is sent. It sends the action, the system and the movement as one request (`Transport.applyDraft`): refused whole, or stopped by another decision, as a movement plan is. The draft is then gone.
- A draft that the game no longer plays (the system got a command token, a ship is gone) keeps its script: the screen says which part does not fit, and Apply is disabled.
- The draft goes on as far as the game is certain: from the movement to the landings and to the production. A step that is not drafted is decided in Live. A step in view stays in view while it is changed; its main button goes on. The tabs open every step that the game came to with the draft. A change of an earlier step keeps the later steps, and the game checks them again.
- The draft ends where something random lies ahead: a roll (a gravity rift, space cannon, a combat), a card that is drawn (a landing on a planet that nobody explored), ships of another seat in the system. The footer says so. This ends the check of the draft, not the draft: Apply stays possible, and the game goes on in Live. Apply sends the steps that the game played; what the draft holds after that point is staged again in Live when its step opens, and nothing of it is sent.
- A production of a draft is in the draft when it is paid. Until then the build is kept, the footer says so, and the game is not asked for it.
- What an ability of the seat asks before a step (AI Development Algorithm) is a list in the draft too. Its main button is "Record": the answer is a part of the draft.
- The game asks "negotiate, or continue" between the steps of a tactical action when the seat has a neighbour. A plan that still has steps continues; so does a draft.
- The Dev sheet has the row "Multiplayer", after "Start from". It loads the address of the quick start, on the seed of the page (`?online&fill=random&seed=<seed>`; the Online play section of `online.md`). The row says the seed and the seats; what it does is behind "?". A page load, not a game choice: the local game stays saved under its own address.
- The Dev sheet has "Other seats": Run (the default) or Step. With Step the game waits before each decision of a seat that is not played here; the panel names the seat, and the main button "Step" lets it decide. The seats decide the same either way, so the saved game does not change. The setting is of the page (`STEPPING_KEY`), for testing: a draft can be opened while another seat has the turn. A reload plays the saved decisions again and then waits at the same step.
- The Dev sheet has "Play as": one row for each seat, with "✓ Playing" or "Random". A row of a random seat is the control: that seat is played here from now on, and the seat that the page was shown as decides at random, from its open decision on (`Transport.capabilities.switchSeat`). With a mask of several seats only the shown seat is given up. The staged movement and the private draft of the seat that is left are dropped. Undo does not go back before the change: to play the old seat again, choose it (owner, 2026-10-10). The changes are part of the saved game (`switches`: the number of the decision and the seats), so a reload, Export and Import keep them.
- In local play the game decides when a ship uses Gravity Drive: only when the ship cannot arrive without it. The toggle is then on and locked. The free toggle of the Movement section of `tactical.md` is not offered there.
- The draft of a secondary (the Strategy cards section of `strategy-cards.md`) is a script too: the answers by their ids (`src/session/secondaryDraft.ts`). The engine plays it with the scope of the secondary (`Transport.draft(script, scope)`), and "ready" sends it with `Transport.applyDraft`. It is kept as the draft of a tactical action is (slot "secondary": `secondaryKey` in a local game, the server online). In a local game the page is shown the game only when a seat of the page is asked, so a secondary is drafted before the seat is reached only with "Other seats: Step", or with several seats played here. A reload goes on at the same decision of the game.
- The game is saved in `localStorage` as the server keeps a game (owner, 2026-10-13): its seed, and the option id of every decision of every seat, in order (`SavedGame.decisions`, format 3, `src/session/savedGame.ts`), with the table that the game was set up as and its wait. Export and Import keep all of it. A reload and an undo play it again from the seed: the engine is given each saved decision, whoever the seat is, so no bot decides again and no seat waits, and the game goes on where it was. The transport learns what the other seats decided from the engine (`ti4_journal`) and keeps the places of the page's own answers in memory (`own`): an undo cuts the list at the answer that it takes back, with what the other seats decided after it. The Dev sheet says both counts ("3 answers saved · 57 decisions of all seats").
- Formats 1 and 2 had the answers of the page only. They are not read: the page says so in place of the game, with "New game". A game that another build of the engine saved may not play again (the game asks another question, or offers other options): the page says that the save is of another engine before it plays it.
- The staged movement of the open movement step is saved too (`draftKey`), with the number of its choice. After a reload it is staged again, so its systems keep their check marks.
- The details, the measurements and the limits are in `crates/ti4-wasm/README.md`.
